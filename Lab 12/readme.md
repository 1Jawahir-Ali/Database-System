# LAB 12 — Aggregate Functions

## Purpose
The purpose of this lab is to analyze and summarize database records using SQL aggregate functions and grouped queries.

## Topics Covered
- COUNT
- DISTINCT
- AVG
- MIN
- MAX
- SUM
- GROUP BY
- HAVING
- ORDER BY
- LIMIT
- Aggregate functions with JOINs

## Work Performed
- Created a University database
- Counted students and courses
- Calculated average, minimum, and maximum marks
- Grouped students by city
- Counted courses by department
- Calculated course enrollment statistics
- Calculated department-wise fee revenue
- Identified students based on their highest marks
- Retrieved top students based on total fees

## Objective
To understand how aggregate functions and grouped queries are used to summarize, analyze, and extract meaningful information from relational data.
